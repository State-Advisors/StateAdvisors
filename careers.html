We are hiring

Two roles are open: canvassers who set the conversation, and salesmen who sit down with the household after that conversation is on the calendar.

Canvasser

You work outside, in neighborhoods we assign. You introduce StateAdvisors, talk through the electric bill and the roof at a high level, and set a time for a salesman to visit. You keep a simple record of who you spoke with.

You need a reliable way to get to the neighborhood, comfort spending the day on your feet, and a plain way of speaking. A no is part of the day. Door-to-door or outside sales experience is useful and is not required. We teach the conversation before you go out on your own.

Salesman

You meet homeowners at appointments that are already set. You walk through the bill, the roof, and whether a purchase or a lease fits. You answer what they ask before anyone is asked to decide. You need a driver license and a car, because the visit is at the home. Evenings and weekends come up, because that is when many households are there.

Sales experience helps, including home-improvement or in-home work. It is not required. What we look for is someone who can explain a number without rushing, take coaching, and show up when the appointment says.

Pay is discussed on the call. Send your name and phone and we will tell you what the week looks like.
