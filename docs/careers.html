We are hiring

Two roles are open. A canvasser starts the conversation at the door. A salesman sits down with the household once that visit is on the calendar.

Canvasser

You are the first person from Solar Solutions a household meets. You work outside, in a neighborhood we assign. You introduce us, talk through the electric bill and the roof in plain terms, and set a time for a salesman to come to the house. You keep a short note on who you spoke with, so the visit starts with the right name and address.

You need a reliable way to get to the neighborhood, and the day is spent on your feet. People will say no, and that is part of the work. Door-to-door experience helps. We teach the conversation before you go out on your own.

Salesman

You are the person who sits down with the household. The appointment is already set. You walk through the bill, look at the roof, and explain whether a purchase or a lease fits this home. You answer their questions before anyone is asked to decide.

The visit is at the house, so you need a driver license and a car. Evenings and weekends come up, because that is when many families are home. Experience selling into homes helps. We look for someone who can explain a number without rushing, take coaching, and be there when the appointment says.

Pay is discussed on the call. Send your name and phone and we will tell you what the week looks like.
