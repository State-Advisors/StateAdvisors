Customer Care

A warm welcome from our team. We are neighbors first, solar advisors second — and we stay with you from the first photo of your home through a finished project.

How we take care of you

If you received an email from us, the green button in that message is the place to share photos of your home, your electric meter, and your utility bills. You do not need an account or a password. This page is here if you want to know who is asking.

A local partner, not a distant call center

StateAdvisors Solar works closely with families and small businesses in communities across the United States. We design around your roof, your bill, and the people who live next door. When you send photos, your advisor, their manager, and our leadership can all see them right away so nobody has to ask twice.

What happens after you upload

Your files help us check your meter, understand your usage, and sketch a design that belongs on your street. If you need to replace a photo, open the same email link, delete a file, and add a new one. That link still works if you find the email again in the next two months.

Talk with us

Questions about your project, your files, or how solar works in your town? Call (347) 308-4256 or email StateSolarAdvisors@gmail.com. We are glad you are here.
