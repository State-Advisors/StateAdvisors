Our process

A working outline of how a project moves with us.

1

First look

We start with the address, the bill, and a conversation about how you use your power. We continue to define your expectations from a solar provider and provide enlightenment on our services. Together, we reached the conclusion on whether this project is worth pursuing.

2

Design and numbers

The system is sized to the building's use of power. Physical inspections and structural calculations are part of this step. We provide a clear statement of the benefits, which we stand by. Finalizing the details allows us to put details in writing for you.

3

Permits and installation

We handle every cost associated with the work. There are no surprises. You can follow our process in the customer portal, and reach us anytime.

4

Turn-on and after

We take on the legal side of the project, so you can handle the daily work at your business or your home. We take you through permission to operate. Service and warranty come through us, not a third party. We remain your point of contact long after we are off the roof.
