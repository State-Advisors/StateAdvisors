Our process

A working outline of how a project moves with us.

1

First look

We start with the address, the bill, and a conversation about how the place uses power. We stay with that until the shape of the work is clear. From there, we reach a conclusion on whether this project is worth pursuing.

2

Design and numbers

The system is sized to the building's use of power. Physical inspections and structural calculations are part of this step. The commitment is a clear statement of the benefits, and we stand by it. Finalizing the project is what lets us assess the needs of your business or your home.

3

Permits and installation

We handle every cost associated with the work. There are no surprises. You can follow our process in the customer portal, and you can reach us anytime.

4

Turn-on and after

We take on the legal side of the project, so you can handle the daily work at your business or your home. We take you through permission to operate. Service and warranty come through us, not a third party. We remain your point of contact long after we are off the roof.
