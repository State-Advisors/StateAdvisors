Zillow looked at houses that sold with solar and similar houses that sold without it. The solar houses went for more. In Zillow's own count the difference was 4.1% across the country, and 5.4% around New York. A later study of sales listed on Zillow put the national difference at about 7%.

They matched the houses on what you can see: bedrooms, bathrooms, size, age, and the place. The years in Zillow's count ran from March 2018 through February 2019. The gap was not the same in every city. Riverside was lower. Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Orlando sat near the national number.

The reason they give is the power the next owner will not have to buy. Someone who uses a lot of electricity can see that and pay more for the house now. Zillow also says a solar house may have other features that are hard to pick out of the price, so the percentage is what the sales showed, not a figure for one address.
