Our process

A working outline of how a project moves with us. The detail of any one roof is settled in the proposal, not on this page.

1

First look

We start with the address, the bill, and a conversation. Photos of the roof and the meter come next, usually from the link we email. That is enough to say whether the site is worth designing.

2

Design and numbers

We size a system to the way the building uses power and put the purchase and lease choices in writing. You see the figure before anyone schedules an install.

3

Permits and installation

We file the permits, meet the inspections, and install. The crew tells you which day they will be on the roof so the building can stay in use.

4

Turn-on and after

The utility's permission to operate is what turns the system on. After that we stay available for production questions, service, and warranty items.
